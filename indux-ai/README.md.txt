# 🏭 InduX AI

### Intelligent Industrial Product Discovery & Analysis Prototype

InduX AI is a lightweight AI-assisted prototype designed to help users discover, analyze, compare, and evaluate industrial products from a large product dataset.

The system combines **semantic product matching, PDF information extraction, product comparison, and data quality analysis** into a simple web application.

> **Hackathon Prototype — Built for demonstrating the concept and core functionality.**

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Finding the right industrial product from a large and unstructured product catalog can be difficult.

Users may need to:

- Search through thousands of products
- Identify products based on natural-language requirements
- Extract useful information from product datasheets
- Compare multiple products
- Check whether product information is complete

Traditional keyword-based searching may return irrelevant products and require users to manually inspect large amounts of information.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**InduX AI** provides a simple product intelligence platform where users can:

1. Describe what type of product they need.
2. Find relevant products using intelligent matching.
3. Upload a product PDF/datasheet and extract important information.
4. Compare multiple products.
5. Analyze the quality and completeness of product data.
